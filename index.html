SFUSD EPC Rules Decoded

How the SFUSD 5th-to-6th Grade Lottery Works

Understanding the district's sequential choice assignment algorithm protects families from common myths (like "bullet voting") and ensures you maximize your 1st-round assignment odds.

Tiebreaker 1

Sibling Attendance

Students with an older brother/sister currently enrolled in 6th or 7th grade who will continue at the middle school next fall.

Tiebreaker 2

Designated Feeder Elementary

Students graduating from official SFUSD designated feeder elementary schools hold prioritized 6th grade rights.

Tiebreaker 3

CTIP1 Census Tract

Students residing in designated Census Tracts with historically lowest average test scores receive priority tiebreaker weight.

Tiebreaker 4

Random Lottery Number

Computer-assigned random number tiebreaker when all other preference weights are identical.

⚠️ The #1 SFUSD Lottery Mistake: "Bullet Voting"

Some parents mistakenly believe listing only 1 school forces SFUSD to assign it. In reality, listing fewer schools gives you zero advantage. If you don't receive your #1 school, SFUSD simply moves down to your #2, #3, etc. Always list every school your family would willingly attend.

What is School Scale & Teaming?

Mega Comprehensive (1,000+ students) & The "House" Teaming Model: Large schools like AP Giannini and Presidio divide 6th graders into dedicated "Houses" or "Cohorts" of ~90 students with shared core teachers. This creates an intimate family feel while offering giant marching bands and deep athletics.
Mid-Sized Middle Schools (450–850 students): Schools like Roosevelt, Hoover, Marina, and Everett offer manageable campuses with strong community identity.
K-8 Alternative & Independent Campuses (<450 students): Intimate cohorts of 40–80 students per grade with low noise and close teacher mentorship.

How the FIT Score is Calculated

The Fit Score is computed out of a strict 100-point budget where your child's personality and passions carry the greatest weight (60% of the total score):

  • Student Personality Archetypes (up to 30 pts): Heavily weighted (+10 pts per selected archetype, divided across up to 3 choices) based on class environment, scale, and sensory support.
  • Favorite Subjects & Passions (up to 30 pts): Heavily weighted (+10 pts per matched subject up to 3) for verified electives like Band, STEM Maker labs, Dual Immersion, or League Athletics.
  • School Scale & Teaming Structure (up to 15 pts): +15 pts for matching preferred Mega House cohorts, Mid-Sized hubs, or K-8/Private continuity (or +7 pts baseline if open to any).
  • Neighborhood / Commute Proximity (up to 15 pts): +15 pts for schools in your preferred transit quadrant (+7 pts if citywide).
  • Target High School Trajectory (up to 10 pts): +10 pts for direct alignment with Lowell, SOTA, or independent prep high school pathways.
  • Base Foundation (5 pts): California middle grades core curriculum baseline.
  • Total maximum possible = 100 points.
